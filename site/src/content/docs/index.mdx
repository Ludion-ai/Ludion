- **For sites:** a free, open-source checkpoint (the Gate) that installs with [3 lines of your code or fewer](/gate) ([measured](https://github.com/Ludion-ai/Ludion/blob/main/docs/MISSION.md#m1-gate)) and first shows which agents came, and what they touched.
- **For agents:** a free identity (the Diver). Signature, status and delegation, carried everywhere after one registration.
- **When something breaks:** Ballast backs who answers. What Ludion verifies is not detection or identity, but accountability.
