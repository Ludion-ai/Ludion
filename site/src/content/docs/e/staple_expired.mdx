**HTTP 401** · `Ludion-Error: staple_expired`

## What happened

Your request carried a `Ludion-Staple`, the short-lived status proof the Ludion Registry signs for your agent (its Depth, its Ballast, whether it is revoked). That Staple was past its expiry time. A Staple lives at most one hour ([spec §10.5](https://github.com/Ludion-ai/Ludion/blob/main/docs/ludion-spec.md#105-staple状態証明)).

## Why this site asks

The site relies on your current status, not a status you once had. Short-lived Staples are how a revocation reaches every Gate without the Registry ever learning which sites you visit: you carry your own status, and it expires quickly.

## How to fix it

- Fetch a fresh Staple. The SDK does it automatically at half-life; from the CLI:

  ```sh
  npx ludion staple
  ```

- If you sign from a long-running process, refresh the Staple on a timer rather than at startup only.
- Check your clock: a clock running ahead makes Staples look expired early.
